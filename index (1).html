About Our School

Building Tomorrow's Leaders Since 1983

New Light High School has been a cornerstone of quality primary and elementary education in Joar Shahara, Dhaka for over four decades. We believe every child deserves a bright start — and we deliver it every day.

  • Established in 1983 — one of the most trusted schools in the area
  • Over 700 students currently enrolled across all grades
  • Recognized by the Bangladesh Education Board
  • Both Bangla and English medium instruction available
  • Co-curricular programs: arts, sports, debate, and tech clubs

    Admissions 2025–26

    Simple Steps to Join Our Family

    Seats are limited. Secure your child's future today.

    Admission Process

    1

    Collect Application Form

    Visit the school office or download the form below. No fee for collecting the form.

    2

    Submit Documents

    Birth certificate, previous report card, 2 passport photos, and parent's NID copy.

    3

    Admission Test

    A short written assessment in Bangla, English & Math (Class 2 and above). Results same day.

    4

    Pay Fees & Confirm Seat

    Confirm your child's seat by paying the admission fee at the school office.

    Important Dates

    Application Deadline: July 31, 2025
    Admission Tests: Every Saturday, 9AM–11AM
    Class Begins: August 15, 2025
